The University of Sydney hosted a memorial event on November 17th, 2026, in PNR 302.

## Description

Bob Kummerfeld passed away on 2 June 2026. Please join us in celebrating his career and contributions.

Bob arrived at the University of Sydney as a student in 1968 and never left. Over more than five decades he helped connect Australia to the internet, built the networks that first linked Australian universities, and shaped the School of Computer Science as a researcher, teacher and twice Head of School. His first task as a lecturer in 1978 was to create the university’s first introduction to computing course. His last project, nearly fifty years later, was the same course, reimagined for a new generation of students.

We’ll gather at the University of Sydney on Tuesday 17 November, 5:30pm to honour Bob’s career, with reflections from colleagues and a panel discussion on technology and innovation, followed by refreshments.

Panellists

Aaron Quigley is Dean of the College of Systems and Society at the Australian National University. He works in human–computer interaction and has held roles at CSIRO, UNSW, the University of St Andrews, University College Dublin and the University of Sydney, where he worked with Bob as a postdoc.

Mike Seymour is a researcher, educator and media and entertainment specialist whose work connects AI and digital humans. He is a Senior Lecturer at the University of Sydney Business School and co-founder of Motus Lab, and completed his BSc, MBA and PhD at Sydney.

Michela Ledwidge is a hands-on director and co-founder of the indie studio Mod, specialising in real-time and virtual production. Her work spans everything from Commodore 64 BASIC games to modern banking AI, and her current focus is VR-optional, story-driven games.
